# Analysis and Research Pipeline

The one-button pipeline freezes the ruleset, source identity, seeds, environment, and output inventory before calculation. It generates a canonical exact model, ten independent million-game optimal simulations, policy comparisons, exact-state summaries, simulation summaries, source datasets for every figure, a factual evidence dossier, and a checksummed research bundle.

The output is evidence for later scientific writing. It does not generate the paper's abstract, discussion, interpretation, novelty statement, or conclusion.

## Canonical experiment

- Rules: `swedish-alga-free-order-v1`
- Exact states: 1,430,528
- Optimal simulations: 10 × 1,000,000
- Comparison policies: immediate greedy, one-turn optimizer, upper-bonus priority, fixed priority, random legal
- Comparison games: 1,000,000 per policy
- Complete game summaries: retained for every game
- Detailed traces: deterministic regular sample and event-triggered examples
- Formats: binary, JSON, CSV, CSV.GZ, SVG, PNG, HTML, PDF, ZIP

## Main analyses

- exact expectation and certified interval;
- independent-build determinism;
- simulation convergence and between-run reproducibility;
- full score distribution and tail probabilities;
- category scores, zeros, timing, and correlations;
- upper-bonus outcomes and exact shadow values;
- Yatzy and combination analyses;
- turn-by-turn trajectories;
- decision margins, ties, keeper patterns, and reroll values;
- category option values and strategic surprises;
- paired policy comparisons and regret;
- computational performance and provenance.
